About Garden

Garden is a free planting guide for home gardeners in India. It shows what to sow in each month in three broad regions, with a growing page for every common vegetable and short guides to pots, seeds, compost and pests.

Where the content comes from

The sowing months are the usual windows followed by gardeners and recommended in general by agricultural universities and state horticulture departments for each climate. They are grouped into three broad regions, so they are a guide, not a rule: your town may be a few weeks earlier or later, and varieties differ. Your local nursery, Krishi Vigyan Kendra or agricultural university can confirm the dates for your district. All text, tips and tables are written for this site and nothing is copied from other sources. Pages were last reviewed in November 2026.

What this site is not

It is a guide for kitchen gardens, balconies and terraces, not a farming advisory, and it gives no advice on pesticides.
